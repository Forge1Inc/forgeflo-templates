Services

The service menu

Every service begins with a personal consultation, so recommendations and timing reflect your hair — not a formula. The menu is kept deliberately focused.

  1. Consultation & Hair Diagnosis

    A one-to-one conversation about your hair's history, texture and ambitions — the starting point of every visit.

  2. Precision Cut & Finish

    Sculpted to your features and finished for movement, longevity and effortless day-to-day wear.

  3. Dimensional Colour

    Full colour, glossing and tonal work with a naturally rich, light-catching finish.

  4. Balayage & Lived-In Lightening

    Hand-painted light and depth, placed where the sun would find it, growing out gracefully.

  5. Treatments & Hair Health

    Restorative rituals to strengthen, smooth and bring back lasting shine.

  6. Occasion & Editorial Styling

    Considered styling for weddings, events and photography — polished, secure and photogenic.

Timings and recommendations are confirmed at consultation, so every appointment is planned around your hair.
